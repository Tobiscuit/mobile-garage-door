# Design — red and white, math-driven, native CSS first

## 1. Persona, and what it decided

I worked as a senior UI/UX designer who specialises in **local home services**: repair and installation trades whose customers call in a hurry and hire on trust. The site told me who those customers are (see `spec.md` §Audience):

| The customer | Their moment | Design decision it drove |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowner with a stuck door, spring or opener | On a phone, one hand, possibly standing in the garage; stressed | **Call and request are one thumb away on every page.** On phones a fixed bottom action bar holds *Call 832-419-1293* and *Request service* in the thumb zone, and the header has a 48 px call button. The hero repeats both as its first actions. No link that matters is smaller than 48 × 48 CSS px. |
| The same homeowner, deciding whether to trust a stranger with their house | Scanning for "are they real, are they local?" | **Proof in plain sight, in this order:** the real logo, "Since 2000", the Houston service areas, the visible phone number, customer testimonials. Fake "live" dashboards are removed because they cost trust (see `copy.md`). |
| Builder or general contractor | Laptop, planning, comparing vendors | A **distinct, secondary path** in the hero (an `h2` panel, "For builders & contractors") leading to projects and the builder portal, without competing with the emergency path. |
| Spanish- or Vietnamese-speaking homeowner | Same urgency, another language | Every string is localized, including the header, footer and form labels that were previously hard-coded in English. Layouts are tested with the longest locale strings (Vietnamese). |

**Brand voice**, taken from the business's own settings (migration `0005`): "a trusted neighbor who happens to be an expert", "confident and reassuring", "avoid fear-mongering". Visually this means calm white space, one strong red, a friendly humanist grotesque (Work Sans, already the site's typeface), no glitch or "techno" motifs, and no pulsing alarms.

## 2. Brand source: the logo

There are two versions of the logo in the repo, both the same flame-ring artwork:

| File | What it is | Added |
|---|---|---|
| `public/images/logos/logo-desktop.svg`, `logo-mobile.svg` | "MOBIL" above a flame ring with "GARAGE DOOR" inside; mobile is "MOBIL" over a flame. Each SVG is a luminance mask over an embedded lossless PNG colour layer; there are no vector fills to read. | 2025-09-25, "Implement actual SVG logos" |
| `public/images/logos/logo.jpg` (1200×896) and `public/icon-512x512.png` | "MOBIL GARAGE DOOR" inside the flame ring. The header renders the JPEG; the PWA icons are lossless PNGs of the same mark. | icons 2026-02-21, JPEG 2026-04-05 |

**Extraction method.** Scripts in the session scratchpad, not committed; the sampling code is summarised here so it can be re-run.
1. Decode the images: `jpeg-js`, `pngjs`, and the base64 PNG layers pulled out of the SVGs.
2. Keep only **interior** pixels: the pixel and all 8 neighbours must be red in OKLCH (chroma ≥ 0.14, 0.38 ≤ L ≤ 0.70, 5° ≤ h ≤ 28°), and for the SVGs the mask must be ≥ 254. This discards anti-aliased edges and JPEG ringing.
3. Take the channel-wise **median** per region: the wordmark and the flame ring separately.

| Source (region) | Interior px | Median | OKLCH | On white |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `icon-512x512.png`, ring mark (lossless) | 14,139 | **`#bb233f`** | 51.8% 0.185 17.4 | 6.13:1 |
| `logo.jpg`, "GARAGE DOOR" | 16,742 | `#b9223f` | 51.4% 0.184 17.0 | 6.24:1 |
| `logo.jpg`, "MOBIL" | 3,450 | `#b1213c` | 49.8% 0.178 17.1 | 6.66:1 |
| `logo-desktop.svg` layer, "MOBIL" and "GARAGE DOOR" (lossless) | 12,307 / 14,478 | **`#cb243c`** | 54.8% 0.199 20.3 | 5.44:1 |
| `logo-desktop.svg` layer, flame ring | 8,343 | `#cc223c` | 54.8% 0.201 20.1 | 5.44:1 |
| `logo-mobile.svg` layer, "MOBIL" | 7,606 | `#c8263b` | 54.3% 0.195 20.7 | 5.53:1 |

**Findings**
- **Two genuine reds.** The ring mark (current logo and app icons) is `#ba233f` (the icon at 512 and 192 px gives `#ba233f`–`#bd243f`). The 2025 SVG lockups use a brighter, warmer `#cb243c`. The JPEG agrees with the lossless icon, so the difference is real, not compression.
- **No third red.** k-means over the JPEG also produced `#91273f`, but the lossless files contain **no interior pixels** darker than L 0.485 in the red band. That cluster is anti-aliasing between the red and navy flames, so it is not treated as a brand colour.
- **Not reds.** The flame's orange (`#da542f`, hue 36°), yellow and blues are separate hues and are excluded by the brief.

**Palette rule.** The logo reds plus white. Where the old CSS drifts (navy `#2c3e50`, yellow `#f1c40f`), **the logo wins** on public pages. The old tokens stay defined for the private dashboard and portal, which are out of scope.

**State colours** are derived at runtime with `color-mix(in oklch, …)` (Baseline widely available since 2025-11), for example `--button-hover: color-mix(in oklch, var(--button-bg), var(--button-fg) 12%)`. One rule then serves red-on-white and white-on-red buttons. **Relative colour syntax is not used**: `color-mix()` covers every state and alpha derivation with wider support.

Semantics that used to rely on other hues are carried by **icon and text, not colour** (WCAG 1.4.1):
- **Success.** The contact confirmation uses a check icon and the words "Request received", not green.
- **Emergency.** It is marked by the word and position, not by red alone, because everything is red.

## 4. Typography

- **Typeface.** **Work Sans** (Wei Huang, SIL OFL), the site's existing face, now **self-hosted** as the variable font (wght 100–900) in three `unicode-range` subsets:
  - latin: 50.3 KB
  - latin-ext: 35.7 KB
  - vietnamese: 11.8 KB

  It ships from `public/fonts/work-sans/` with `OFL.txt`. These are the same files Google Fonts served, so the letterforms don't change.
- **Fallback metrics** (CLS), computed from the font file's `hhea`/`OS/2` (upm 1000, ascent 930, descent −243, lineGap 0), which match `@capsizecss/metrics` 4.2.0, against Arial:
  `size-adjust: 111.9334%; ascent-override: 83.0851%; descent-override: 21.7093%; line-gap-override: 0%`.
  Support differs by descriptor:
  - `size-adjust`: Chrome 92, Firefox 92, Safari 17.
  - `ascent`/`descent`/`line-gap-override`: Chrome 87, Firefox 89, **Safari not yet** (BCD 8.1.1). A browser that doesn't know a descriptor ignores it (CSS Syntax: invalid declarations are dropped), so Safari falls back to plain Arial metrics. That is the pre-existing behaviour, not a regression.
- **Wordmark.** The header lockup echoes the logo: the logo's "MOBIL" is a bold italic serif, "GARAGE DOOR" a bold grotesque. The text wordmark sets *Mobil* in `font-family: "Times New Roman", Times, serif; font-style: italic` (a system font, zero bytes) and "Garage Door" in Work Sans 700.
- **Weights.** Headings 800 with `letter-spacing: -0.02em` at display sizes; body 400; UI labels 600.
- **Line height.**
  - Body 1.6: within Bringhurst's 1.4–1.6 range for 60–70-character lines, and ≥ 1.5 so text meets WCAG 1.4.12 without user overrides.
  - Steps 1–3: 1.2. Display: 1.05.
- **Measure.** `--measure: 65ch` for running text (the 45–75-character comfort range).

### 4.1 Fluid modular type scale
Two inputs per end of the range, interpolated linearly between a **390 px** phone and a **1440 px** laptop (`clamp()`, rem + vw so browser zoom still scales the rem part):

| | 390 px | 1440 px |
|---|---|---|
| base (step 0) | 16 px (no iOS focus zoom; comfortable body) | 18 px |
| ratio | **6:5 (minor third)** | **4:3 (perfect fourth)** |

**Why two ratios.**
- **Phones.** A 390 px column can't hold a strong hierarchy: a fourth-based h1 of 16 × (4/3)⁵ = 67 px would break "installation" and long Vietnamese words mid-line. The minor third keeps headings distinct but narrow.
- **Laptops.** A laptop can afford, and benefits from, the steeper fourth for scanning.
- **Where φ comes in.** The fourth's fifth step lands at (4/3)⁵ = 4.214, within 0.5% of **φ³ = 4.236**. The laptop hero headline therefore stands to body text in golden proportion cubed, without forcing φ onto every step, where φ⁴ = 6.85× would be absurd.

| Token | 390 px | 1440 px | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--step-n1` | 13.33 px | 13.50 px | captions, legal small print |
| `--step-0` | 16.00 | 18.00 | body |
| `--step-1` | 19.20 | 24.00 | lead paragraphs, card titles |
| `--step-2` | 23.04 | 32.00 | h3 |
| `--step-3` | 27.65 | 42.67 | h2 |
| `--step-4` | 33.18 | 56.89 | page h1 |
| `--step-5` | 39.81 | 75.85 | — |
| `--step-display` | 33.18 | 75.85 | home hero h1 (spans steps 4 → 5) |

Step −2 is not defined: with these ratios it would *shrink* as the viewport grows (11.1 → 10.1 px). The checker rejects that.

## 5. Spacing and layout

### 5.1 Spacing scale: powers of φ
Spacing needs fewer, more clearly distinct steps than type. Each step is **φ × the previous one** (62% larger), so neighbouring gaps never look accidentally "almost equal". The base (`s`) is the body size, so spacing breathes with the text.

| Token | Power | 390 px | 1440 px |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--space-3xs` | φ⁻³ | 3.78 px | 4.25 px |
| `--space-2xs` | φ⁻² | 6.11 | 6.88 |
| `--space-xs` | φ⁻¹ | 9.89 | 11.12 |
| `--space-s` | φ⁰ | 16.00 | 18.00 |
| `--space-m` | φ¹ | 25.89 | 29.12 |
| `--space-l` | φ² | 41.89 | 47.12 |
| `--space-xl` | φ³ | 67.78 | 76.25 |
| `--space-2xl` | φ⁴ | 109.67 | 123.37 |

**Fluid pairs** step up a level across the range, for sections and gutters: `--space-s-m` (16 → 29.1), `--space-m-l` (25.9 → 47.1), `--space-l-xl` (41.9 → 76.3), `--space-xl-2xl` (67.8 → 123.4).

- **Page gutter** `--space-s-m`.
- **Section padding** `--space-xl-2xl`.
- **Card padding** `--space-m`.
- **Stack gap** in text `--space-s`.
- **Radii come from the same scale.** Controls and cards use `--space-xs` (≈ 10–11 px). Chips use `--space-2xs`.

### 5.2 Layout proportion: where φ earns its place
- **Container width = measure × φ**: `min(100% - 2 × gutter, 65ch × 1.618)`, ≈ 1041 px at an 18 px root.
- **Two-column layouts split φ : 1**: `grid-template-columns: 1.618fr 1fr`.
  - The main column is then exactly **one comfortable measure** (≈ 643 px ≈ 65ch).
  - The aside is ≈ 398 px (≈ 40ch), wide enough for a contact card or a trust list.
  - Used by the home hero (copy : proof card), the blog post (article : call-to-action) and the contact page (form : contact card).
- **Open Graph image**: red band height = 630 / φ⁴ ≈ 92 px; logo height = white field ÷ φ ≈ 333 px.
- **Where φ is not used.** Constants that come from outside the scale, each with its source:
  - **Tap targets.** `--tap-min: 3rem` (48 px). WCAG 2.5.8 AA requires 24 px and 2.5.5 AAA 44 px; 48 px is the thumb-friendly size platform guidelines use.
  - **Hairlines.** 1 px.
  - **Input borders.** 2 px, so they meet 3:1 and read as fields.
  - **Focus ring.** 3 px with a 2 px offset (≥ the 2 px perimeter in WCAG 2.4.13).

### 5.3 Checker
`scripts/design-scale.mjs --check` (also run by the unit test in CI) fails if:
1. the generated blocks in `brand-theme.css` or `site.css` differ from the generator;
2. any type or space step fails to strictly increase at any viewport from 390 to 1440 px in 10 px increments;
3. body text drops below 16 px;
4. any contrast pair in §3 falls under its WCAG ratio.

## 6. Native CSS: what is used, where, and its fallback

Status from `web-features` 3.38.0 / `@mdn/browser-compat-data` 8.1.1 (2026-09-10), queried 2026-09-13. The context7 MDN docs were used for syntax.

| Feature | Baseline | Where it earns its place | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cascade layers (`@layer`) | widely (2024-09) | `site.css` joins Tailwind's `components` layer, so utilities still override components and nothing depends on source order | not needed |
| CSS nesting | widely (2026-06) | every component block | not needed |
| Container queries (`container-type: inline-size`, `@container`) | widely (2025-08) | Service, project and blog **cards adapt to the width of their slot**, not the viewport. The same card is a stacked tile in a three-column grid and a side-by-side row in a single column. | not needed |
| `:has()` | widely (2026-06) | 1) `html:has(#site-menu[data-open])` locks page scroll while the mobile menu is open, with no JS. 2) `body:has(.action-bar)` lifts the floating AI button above the mobile action bar. 3) `.field:has(:focus-visible)` highlights the whole field, label included. 4) `.card:has(img)` switches card layout when an image exists. | not needed |
| Subgrid (`grid-template-rows: subgrid`) | widely (2026-03) | Service cards share row tracks, so titles, descriptions, feature lists and CTAs **align across cards** of different text lengths | not needed; without it rows fall back to auto |
| OKLCH + `color-mix()` | widely (2025-11) | the whole palette and every state colour | not needed |
| `text-wrap: balance` | newly (2024-05): Chrome 114, FF 121, Safari 17.5 | headings: no one-word last lines in "Garage door repair and installation in Houston" at any width | `@supports (text-wrap: balance)`; otherwise normal wrapping |
| `text-wrap: pretty` | **limited** (no Firefox) | body paragraphs: avoids orphans | `@supports (text-wrap: pretty)`; otherwise normal wrapping |
| `field-sizing: content` | newly (2026-06) | the contact form's issue textarea grows with what the customer types | `@supports`; otherwise the existing `rows={4}` |
| Scroll-driven animations (`animation-timeline: scroll()`) | **limited** (Chrome 115, Safari 26; Firefox behind a flag) | 1) header shadow appears once the page scrolls, replacing a scroll listener; 2) reading-progress bar on blog posts | `@supports (animation-timeline: scroll())` **and** `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; otherwise a static bottom border and no progress bar |
| `@font-face` metric overrides | `size-adjust` newly; others limited (no Safari) | fallback metrics (§4) | ignored descriptors (CSS Syntax), which is today's behaviour |
| `fetchpriority` | newly (2024-10) | blog hero image | ignored attribute; the image is still eager |
| AVIF `<picture>` + WebP | widely (AVIF 2026-07) | header logo mark | WebP `<img>` inside `<picture>` |

**Considered and not used**
- **`@scope`.** Baseline newly available, Chrome 118 / Firefox 146 / Safari 26.4. The two places it could help don't need it:
  - *Surface theming* (links and buttons inside red vs white sections) is handled by custom properties, which already inherit from the nearest ancestor. That is exactly `@scope`'s proximity behaviour, with universal support.
  - *Donut exclusion* in CMS rich text is covered by `:not(.not-prose *)`.

  The standard guard, `@supports at-rule(@scope)`, is itself only in Chrome 148 per BCD 8.1.1, so it would *hide* `@scope` from Firefox and Safari, which support it.
- **Cross-document view transitions** (`@view-transition`). Limited, no Firefox. The site navigates with full page loads, where they would work, but the new page paints through a cross-fade from opacity 0. web.dev: opacity-0 elements are not LCP candidates. Not worth a slower LCP on every navigation.
- **Relative colour syntax.** See §3: `color-mix()` covers the need with wider support.
- **Dark mode.** The brand is explicitly red on white. No `prefers-color-scheme` theme; `color-scheme: light` is declared so form controls match.

## 7. Accessibility
- **Landmarks.** Skip link (first focusable element, "Skip to main content", localized) → `<main id="main" tabindex="-1">`. The `<header>` has a labelled `<nav>`, the `<footer>` has its own navigation and contact blocks, and no page nests `<main>`.
- **Headings.** One `h1` per page and no skipped levels in main content (see `seo.md` §4.8).
- **Focus.** `:focus-visible` gets a 3 px outline in the surface's focus colour (red on light at 6.18:1, white on red at 6.18:1 and on deep red at 13.49:1), with a 2 px offset. Outlines are never removed.
- **Contrast.** Every text and UI pair is in §3 and checked in CI.
- **Mobile menu.**
  - The button carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
  - The closed panel is `inert` and hidden. Today's closed menu is only `opacity: 0` and its links stay tabbable.
  - Escape closes it.
- **Contact form.**
  - Every input gets a `<label for>` (`AddressAutocomplete` gains an optional `id` prop; its behaviour and payload are unchanged).
  - The Standard / Emergency toggle buttons expose `aria-pressed`.
  - Decorative emoji are removed from strings.
- **Images.** Existing alt text is kept (blog `featuredImage.alt || title`, project titles). The header mark has `alt=""` because the link's visible text already says "Mobil Garage Door" — reading the name twice helps no one. Testimonial stars get a text equivalent ("Rated 5 out of 5").
- **Motion.** All animation sits under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`: hover lifts, the header scroll shadow, the reading progress bar and the loading spinner. The pulsing "ping" dots are removed.
- **Targets.** `--tap-min` 48 px on every interactive element in header, footer, action bar, cards and form.
- **Language.** `<html lang>` per locale (unchanged). Brand and product names don't change between locales.
